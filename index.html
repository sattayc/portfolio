About me

I am Piyathida, an aspiring UX/UI designer and front-end developer. I am a motivated learner with a passion for creating clear, intuitive and meaningful digital experiences. I am currently in my second year of Communication and Multimedia Design at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences (HvA), where I completed my first year Cum Laude. Throughout my studies, I have worked on a variety of projects ranging from designing interactive Figma prototypes to developing fully coded websites. I am a friendly and outgoing person who enjoys collaborating with others, while also being comfortable taking initiative and working independently. I love exploring new ideas, expanding my skills, and pushing myself to grow. I am always open to opportunities where I can contribute, apply my creativity, and further develop my abilities.
